RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 5 March 2027 between:

LANDLORD: Marina Crest Holdings W.L.L., a company registered in the State of Qatar, with its office at Lusail Marina District, Doha (the "Landlord"); and

TENANT: Yusuf Rahman, holder of Qatar ID as recorded with the Landlord (the "Tenant").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord leases to the Tenant Apartment 0301, Tower A, Marina Crest Residences, Lusail Marina District, Doha, a three-bedroom apartment of approximately 156 square metres, together with parking bay A-12 (the "Premises").

2. TERM
The lease is for a fixed term of thirty-six (36) months, commencing on 1 April 2027 and expiring on 31 March 2030.

3. RENT
The annual rent is QAR 168,000, payable in four equal quarterly instalments of QAR 42,000, each due in advance on the first day of the quarter.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of QAR 14,000 on signing this Agreement. The deposit is refundable within thirty days of the end of the term, less any sums lawfully due to the Landlord.

5. RENEWAL
This lease may be renewed for a further term by written agreement of both parties made at least ninety (90) days before the expiry date.

6. EARLY TERMINATION
The Tenant may terminate this lease after the first twelve months by giving ninety (90) days written notice.

7. USE AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall use the Premises as a private residence only and shall keep them in good condition. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and for the air conditioning and water heating systems.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Qatar.

SIGNED by the parties on the date first written above.

For the Tenant: /s/ Yusuf Rahman
