RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 12 May 2027 between:

LANDLORD: Marina Crest Holdings W.L.L., a company registered in the State of Qatar, with its office at Lusail Marina District, Doha (the "Landlord"); and

TENANT: Hana Saleh, holder of Qatar ID as recorded with the Landlord (the "Tenant").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord leases to the Tenant Apartment 1204, Tower C, Marina Crest Residences, Lusail Marina District, Doha, a two-bedroom apartment, together with one parking bay (the "Premises").

2. TERM
The lease is for a fixed term of forty-eight (48) months, commencing on 1 June 2027 and expiring on 31 May 2031.

3. RENT
The annual rent is QAR 180,000, payable in monthly instalments of QAR 15,000, each due in advance on the first day of the month. A parking fee of QAR 300 per month is payable together with the rent. A late payment fee of QAR 500 applies to any instalment paid more than ten days after its due date.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of QAR 15,000 on signing this Agreement. The deposit of QAR 6,000 paid by the previous occupant of the Premises has been refunded and does not form part of this Agreement.

5. RENT ESCALATION
At the end of each lease year the rent shall be adjusted in line with the Qatar Consumer Price Index published by the Planning and Statistics Authority, up to a maximum increase of five percent (5%).

6. RENEWAL
This lease renews automatically for a further term of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the expiry date.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this lease before the expiry date by giving ninety (90) days written notice. A Tenant who terminates early shall pay two months rent as compensation.

8. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Qatar.

SIGNED by the parties on the date first written above.

For the Tenant: /s/ Hana Saleh
