RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 10 February 2027 between:

LANDLORD: Marina Crest Holdings W.L.L., a company registered in the State of Qatar, with its office at Lusail Marina District, Doha (the "Landlord"); and

TENANT: Omar Khalil, holder of Qatar ID as recorded with the Landlord (the "Tenant").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord leases to the Tenant Apartment 1205, Tower B, Marina Crest Residences, Lusail Marina District, Doha, a two-bedroom apartment of approximately 121 square metres, together with parking bay B-78 (the "Premises").

2. TERM
The lease is for a fixed term of twenty-four (24) months, commencing on 1 March 2027 and expiring on 31 August 2028.

3. RENT
The annual rent is QAR 120,000, payable in monthly instalments of QAR 11,000, each due in advance on the first day of the month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of QAR 5,000 on signing this Agreement. The deposit is refundable within thirty days of the end of the term, less any sums lawfully due to the Landlord.

5. RENT REVIEW
The rent may be increased upon renewal as mutually agreed between the parties.

6. RENEWAL
This lease may be renewed for a further term by written agreement of both parties made at least sixty (60) days before the expiry date.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
The Tenant may terminate this lease before the expiry date by giving sixty (60) days written notice and paying one month rent as compensation.

8. USE AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall use the Premises as a private residence only and shall keep them in good condition. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and for the air conditioning and water heating systems.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Qatar.

SIGNED by the parties on the date first written above.
