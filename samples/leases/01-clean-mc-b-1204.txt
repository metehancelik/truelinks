RESIDENTIAL LEASE AGREEMENT

This Lease Agreement is made on 15 December 2026 between:

LANDLORD: Marina Crest Holdings W.L.L., a company registered in the State of Qatar, with its office at Lusail Marina District, Doha (the "Landlord"); and

TENANT: Sara Haddad, holder of Qatar ID as recorded with the Landlord (the "Tenant").

1. PREMISES
The Landlord leases to the Tenant Apartment 1204, Tower B, Marina Crest Residences, Lusail Marina District, Doha, a two-bedroom apartment of approximately 118 square metres, together with parking bay B-77 (the "Premises").

2. TERM
The lease is for a fixed term of twelve (12) months, commencing on 1 January 2027 and expiring on 31 December 2027.

3. RENT
The annual rent is QAR 114,000, payable in twelve equal monthly instalments of QAR 9,500, each due in advance on the first day of the month.

4. SECURITY DEPOSIT
The Tenant shall pay a security deposit of QAR 9,500 on signing this Agreement. The deposit is refundable within thirty days of the end of the term, less any sums lawfully due to the Landlord.

5. RENT ESCALATION
On each renewal of this lease the rent shall increase by five percent (5%) of the rent payable in the preceding term.

6. RENEWAL
This lease renews automatically for a further term of twelve (12) months unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal at least sixty (60) days before the expiry date.

7. EARLY TERMINATION
Either party may terminate this lease before the expiry date by giving ninety (90) days written notice. A Tenant who terminates early shall pay two months rent as compensation.

8. USE AND MAINTENANCE
The Tenant shall use the Premises as a private residence only and shall keep them in good condition. The Landlord is responsible for structural repairs and for the air conditioning and water heating systems.

9. GOVERNING LAW
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Qatar.

SIGNED by the parties on the date first written above.

For the Tenant: /s/ Sara Haddad
